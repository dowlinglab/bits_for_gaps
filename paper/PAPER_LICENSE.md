# Bundled paper: citation and license

`bits_for_gaps_paper.pdf` in this directory is the published article describing the method
implemented by this package. It is included so that the method and the code can be read
side by side.

## Citation

> K. D. Jones and A. W. Dowling, "BITS for GAPS: Bayesian Information-Theoretic Sampling for
> hierarchical GAussian Process Surrogates," *Computers & Chemical Engineering* **211**
> (2026) 109650.
> DOI: [10.1016/j.compchemeng.2026.109650](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compchemeng.2026.109650)

Please cite the DOI — it is the canonical reference. The bundled PDF is a convenience copy.
